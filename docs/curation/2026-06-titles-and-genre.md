**For:** CD / founder. **CC must not invent titles or genres** — every blank below is yours to fill.

## How to fill this in

- **Real title** — fill **only** for the **10 placeholder-titled items** in Section A (the 5
  SoundCloud sets + 5 Various playlists). Their current titles are positional stand-ins
  (`SoundCloud set 1`, `Various playlist 1`, …), not real names. The 18 Vibes in Section B
  already have real titles — leave their title alone.
- **Genre(s)** — fill for **every** row in both sections. Comma-separated, **your own
  vocabulary** (e.g. `house, deep house` or `salsa, latin`). An item can have several genres;
  that's expected and is what lets it surface in more than one place later.
- **Listen** — open the link to hear the set and identify it. Spotify links open the playlist
  page directly. SoundCloud sets open via the widget player (the numeric **Embed id** is the
  SoundCloud set id; SoundCloud no longer serves a clean public page from the bare id, so use
  the player link to listen).
- **Out of scope here:** the **2 Beatport releases** (`rel-1`, `rel-2`) — their title question
  (artwork filename vs. track slug) is being handled separately. Not in this worksheet.

**Totals:** 28 music items — 5 Mixtapes (SoundCloud) + 18 Vibes + 5 Various.
Title needed: **10** (Section A). Genre needed: **all 28**.
